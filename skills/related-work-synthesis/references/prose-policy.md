# Prose editing rules

## Connected reasoning and clear sentences

Use connected paragraphs with concrete subjects and verbs. Remove empty staging and repeated assurances. Check the inference and referents between adjacent sentences. Simplify nested clauses while keeping conditions attached to their claims. Merge repeated propositions serving the same reader purpose, but retain necessary definitions, limitations, and standalone summaries.

## Concepts and terminology

Keep technical terms stable. Resolve the intended concept, scope, and implied properties before changing a term. Separate a wrong concept from awkward expression of a correct one. Check word combinations, grammar, modifiers, and agency in context. Preserve valid specialist terms, defined new concepts, and disciplinary uses even when unfamiliar. For a language-only repair, retain the technical term. Keep a correct passage unchanged and flag uncertain meaning rather than guessing. Verify that replacements preserve units, conditions, distinctions, and evidential force; do not use global word bans or substitutions.

## Evidence in the audit

Apply the same standard to the audit itself. Do not add an unreported mechanism, mathematical form, or property when paraphrasing the source or explaining a finding. Keep an unspecified operation unspecified. For a named experimental condition or author-defined construct, check its operational definition and surrounding table or paragraph before classifying the label. If the operation is correct but the shorthand may mislead, report a naming clarification and consider a local gloss; do not call it a scientific error merely because the label is abbreviated. Professional wording need not be elaborate. An “AI feel” is an editing concern, not an authorship finding.

## Punctuation and task scope

This library defaults to no explanatory colons or sentence-interrupting dashes in generated prose, including spaced hyphens and LaTeX dash syntax. Preserve compound-word hyphens, notation, minus signs, ranges, ratios, URLs, citations, exact quotations, and required templates. Author or venue instructions take precedence. Do not replace paragraphs with bullet fragments, mechanically erase punctuation, or impose a sentence-length quota. Apply these checks only within the requested task; a source or submission check does not itself authorize prose edits.
