# Worked example: archive indexing

This scenario and its numbers are fictional.

**Request:** “Turn these outcomes into a concise Results paragraph.”

**Evidence:** An indexing pipeline recovered every reference entry in 39/54 archive folders, compared with 30/54 for a preset comparator. The full pipeline's supplied 95% interval is [0.58, 0.84], with archive collections as the resampling unit. No between-method analysis is supplied. Within the same full-pipeline runs, 28 folders completed initially and 11 completed after retrying. A separate collection of damaged folders produced 16/32 versus 11/32 completions. The separate collection differs in source archives and document quality. An internal validation study reports 0.66, 0.69, and 0.67 across joint settings of two scoring coefficients; no feature is removed.

**Tempting interpretation:** “Retries caused an 11-folder improvement. Damage cost exactly 22.2 percentage points. Sensitivity proves the scoring feature explains the gain.”

**Why those statements fail:** The first-pass count describes membership in the same runs, not an independently executed intervention. The separate archive collection changes more than damage. Coefficient sensitivity retains the feature and does not isolate its contribution. None of these limits erases the measured completion outcomes.

**Supported paragraph:** “The full pipeline recovered every reference entry in 39/54 folders, compared with 30/54 for the preset comparator. Its supplied 95% interval was 58–84%, resampling archive collections. Of its 39 completed folders, 28 completed initially and 11 after retries. On the separate damaged-folder collection, completion was 16/32 versus 11/32. The full pipeline therefore had higher observed completion in both collections; the collection differences do not isolate a damage effect. Internal validation rates ranged from 0.66 to 0.69 across joint coefficient settings.”

**What this keeps:** The main comparison, uncertainty method, observed role of retries, separate difficult-condition evidence, and measured sensitivity. It does not claim a between-method significance result or a causal contribution for retries or the scoring feature. A user who requested only the primary result need not receive every secondary finding.
