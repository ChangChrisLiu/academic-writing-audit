# Academic Writing Audit

Make the research argument easier to follow while preserving its evidential meaning and the author's voice. Remove unnecessary self-defense; retain the conditions needed to understand the result.

## Establish the task

Identify the current source, requested scope, and mode: **audit**, **targeted revision**, or **final check**. An audit request returns findings before rewriting. An explicit revision request authorizes the bounded edit; do not add an approval gate. Honor narrower instructions.

Read the relevant passage in context. Form an independent account of its question, contribution, reasoning, and evidence before consulting earlier judgments. Prior reviews locate concerns; they do not establish that the current text is correct. Separate current source facts, author decisions, inspected evidence, and unresolved assertions. With an excerpt, limit the verdict to that excerpt.

## Choose expertise and define the expected logic

For a scientific or reviewer-informed audit, read [roles and expectations](references/roles-and-expectations.md). Match the reviewing lens to the discipline, subfield, study design, audience, and actual judgment required. Separate the scientific reviewer's validity question from the editor's expression question; additional specialists or agents are optional, not a prerequisite.

For each consequential issue, identify the substantive question, what the argument or evidence needs to establish and why, what the current passage establishes, and the remaining difference. Label actual review requests separately from inferred reader needs. Do not demand a stronger result than the retained claim requires. A mention of the right topic or a confident response letter is not proof that the question is answered.

## Review in this order

1. **Argument:** Reconstruct problem → gap → contribution → mechanism or reasoning → evidence → conclusion. Preserve complementary contributions. Identify missing transitions between objects or stages. Read [argument and evidence](references/argument-and-evidence.md) for a full scientific audit.
2. **Evidence:** For consequential claims, identify the supporting source, study or argument, comparison, outcome, and permitted inference. Separate observation, interpretation, and causal attribution. Define baselines by their actual operations. Keep distinct studies, versions, and evaluation endpoints distinct.
3. **Reader function:** Ask what each questionable sentence contributes: motivation, definition, mechanism, comparison, result, interpretation, boundary, or navigation. Assess the fact's importance, its formulation, and its location separately. Use [nondefensive writing](references/nondefensive-writing.md) when reducing defensive tone.
4. **Revision:** Use the smallest change that repairs a named problem. Preserve a coherent motivation-to-contribution passage verbatim when no concrete defect is identified. Deleting its gap sentence merely because it is broad can destroy the reason the work exists. Concision is not a sentence quota.
5. **Prose:** Check concrete subjects, sentence-to-sentence reasoning, clause complexity, repeated propositions, and the prose preference below. Do not strengthen a claim to make it sound more direct. Read [prose checks](references/prose-checks.md) for a whole-passage clarity pass.
6. **Verification:** Re-read the connected passage and check the diff against the evidence. Use [review and patching](references/review-and-patching.md) for file edits, reviewer-informed revision, and final checks. Use [worked examples](references/examples.md) for ambiguous keep/delete decisions.

## Check the manuscript's progression

Assess whether the author understands a specific question before announcing a method. Generic statements about a field's importance do not establish the study's need. An abstract should connect the concrete task, existing route's limitation, unresolved difficulty, contribution, and principal finding. An introduction should narrow from the relevant direction and its progress to the gap, motivation, and supported contribution. Preserve an already coherent argument and adapt to a required abstract format.

Derive a small number of connected literature threads, usually two or three, from that introduction. The review explains prior studies and their relationships; it should not default to separate camps, a paper list, or repeated comparisons with the present paper. Put the current study's positioning in the introduction or discussion unless an explicit comparison task requires otherwise. Methods should follow dependency order without repeating definitions and assurances. Results, limitations, and conclusion must follow the discipline, design, and supplied section conventions. Check concise connected reasoning rather than requiring lists or a fixed paragraph count.

## Make the decision explicit

| Decision | Apply when |
| --- | --- |
| Keep | The sentence performs a necessary, distinct job and reads clearly. |
| Clarify | A definition, bridge, or inference is missing or misleading. |
| Merge | Two passages repeat the same fact for the same reader purpose. |
| Move | The fact belongs elsewhere; the destination can carry it without hiding an essential condition. |
| Delete | The passage adds reassurance, rebuttal staging, or repetition without a necessary reader function. |

Necessary negation, eligibility rules, uncertainty, unequal budgets, and genuine limitations are not defects in tone. Conversely, an important fact does not justify every wording or every repetition. Explain an operation before referring to its parameter appendix.

## Prose when drafting or revising

Use connected paragraphs with concrete subjects and verbs. Remove empty staging and repeated assurances. Check the inference and referents between adjacent sentences. Simplify nested clauses while keeping conditions attached to their claims. Merge repeated propositions serving the same reader purpose, but retain necessary definitions, limitations, and standalone summaries. Keep technical terms stable. Resolve the intended concept, scope, and implied properties before changing a term. Separate a wrong concept from awkward expression of a correct one. Check word combinations, grammar, modifiers, and agency in context. Preserve valid specialist terms, defined new concepts, and disciplinary uses even when unfamiliar. For a language-only repair, retain the technical term. Keep a correct passage unchanged and flag uncertain meaning rather than guessing. Verify that replacements preserve units, conditions, distinctions, and evidential force; do not use global word bans or substitutions. Apply the same standard to the audit itself. Do not add an unreported mechanism, mathematical form, or property when paraphrasing the source or explaining a finding. Keep an unspecified operation unspecified. For a named experimental condition or author-defined construct, check its operational definition and surrounding table or paragraph before classifying the label. If the operation is correct but the shorthand may mislead, report a naming clarification and consider a local gloss; do not call it a scientific error merely because the label is abbreviated. Professional wording need not be elaborate. An “AI feel” is an editing concern, not an authorship finding.

This library defaults to no explanatory colons or sentence-interrupting dashes in generated prose, including spaced hyphens and LaTeX dash syntax. Preserve compound-word hyphens, notation, minus signs, ranges, ratios, URLs, citations, exact quotations, and required templates. Author or venue instructions take precedence. Do not replace paragraphs with bullet fragments, mechanically erase punctuation, or impose a sentence-length quota. Apply these checks only within the requested task; a source or submission check does not itself authorize prose edits.

## Deliver a bounded result

For an audit, provide a short overall judgment, prioritized findings with locations and evidence, explicit keep decisions, and material unresolved facts. Distinguish scientific errors, missing explanations, and optional style preferences.

For a revision, provide the replacement or patch, why it is needed, and what was preserved. Flag a substantive evidence conflict instead of inventing a reconciliation. Report source inspection, compilation, rendering, and external publication separately, and only when observed.

Stop when the requested problems are resolved and the argument remains intact. A section with no justified change is a successful outcome. Do not expand a writing pass into new experiments, exhaustive audit paperwork, or a wholesale rewrite. These are reasoning roles for one agent; extra agents, particular models, and external services are optional and require the user's authorized scope.
