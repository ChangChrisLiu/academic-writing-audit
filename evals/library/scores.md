## Interpretation of these judgments

The controls' competent responses limit what can be inferred about added value. The clearer preservation marker and transfer wording in some guided answers are observations from individual responses, not evidence of a stable improvement. These cases are useful starting points for regression checks; harder held-out cases and actual tool workflows remain untested.
