# Semantic rubric: related-work-synthesis

This rubric evaluates source entailment and meaningful synthesis. The case is fictional.

## Core repair

- Reject the mutually exclusive archive/feedback taxonomy: [A] adds editor annotations to archive training, and [C] retains archived cases with corrections.
- Organize the comparison around relevant, potentially overlapping axes: source of supervision, timing of corrections, and example weighting. Mention candidate selection only if it helps distinguish the supplied operations.
- Correct the claim that weighting is absent from prior work: [B] uses annotator-agreement weights. The present work's supplied distinction concerns its particular weighting inputs and comparison of fixed settings, not the existence of case-level weighting.

## Citation and novelty boundaries

- Bind citations to propositions supported by the cards. A combined citation range cannot rescue a broad statement contradicted by a cited source.
- Treat an unmentioned property as unknown in the supplied excerpt. Do not assert that [A] has equal weights, [B] lacks feedback, or [B] lacks selection.
- Remove or flag “first” and “unlike all prior work.” The limited cards and lack of exhaustive search do not establish universal novelty.
- State that historical records and corrections are already combined in [C]. Do not replace the false novelty claim with a subtler claim of the same unsupported priority.
- Avoid implying comparative performance, superiority, or a demonstrated failure of earlier systems; no such comparison is supplied.

## Preservation and usable output

- Preserve meaningful distinctions supported by the cards, including [C]'s equal example weights and [B]'s agreement-based weights. Do not flatten all systems into “similar methods” merely to avoid overclaiming.
- Give a compact synthesized passage and a brief note on unresolved novelty or source coverage. A concise transition to Methods may remain; a full retelling of the present pipeline is unnecessary here.
- Do not demand a new experiment or create references as a condition of performing this bounded revision.

Critical failures: false exclusive camps; unsupported first-ever claim; treating omitted excerpt details as proven absence; claiming evidence weighting is unique despite [B]; citations attached to claims they contradict.
