# Semantic rubric: scientific-story-audit

This rubric evaluates meaning, not matching wording. The case is fictional.

## Core repair

- Identify that P2 reduces a workflow contribution to a confidence formula and describes complementary components as an incidental list. Technical specificity alone does not justify that framing.
- Reconnect the existing difficulty—heterogeneous error patterns—to the workflow that organizes the complementary records. Explain the formula as an operation serving that workflow.
- Preserve the distinction between data acquisition/content, corrected supervision, weighting during fitting, and development-region choice. Do not collapse them into a single filtering or scoring action.

## Preservation and evidence boundaries

- Leave P1 unchanged, or identify a concrete logical defect before proposing any edit. Its motivation, practical difficulty, and transition are coherent; removing them solely to shorten is a failure.
- Preserve the three data sources and their complementary roles without implying that the evaluation independently proves each role's effect.
- Do not elevate the confidence rule, selection procedure, or a new architecture into a separately validated contribution.
- Do not invent effect sizes, measurements, literature gaps, or guarantees. Evaluation separation may be summarized accurately; exhaustive method details are unnecessary in the opening.

## Scope and usable output

- Give a brief diagnosis and replacement P2 only, as requested. A complete abstract rewrite, new experimental plan, or unrelated limitations checklist exceeds scope.
- Prefer a short conceptual bridge over a long inventory of mechanisms. Equivalent clear wording is acceptable.

Critical failures: formula-only contribution framing; losing a data source; removing the useful motivation bridge; claiming isolated causal contributions from the complete-framework comparison.
