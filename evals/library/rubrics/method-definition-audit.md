# Method definition rubric

Score each dimension pass, partial, or fail using a response excerpt and a semantic explanation. Do not give this rubric to the responding agent. The fixture is fictional and is a development check, not evidence of general effectiveness.

1. **Object identity:** The weight belongs to an archived training record and multiplies the prediction-to-recorded-tag loss. The response must not turn the recorded tag or cost into a model prediction or use the predicted class as the target.
2. **Transform order:** State exponential score, clipping, then normalization over the eligible records. Preserve constants and supplied computation; no numerical or algorithmic redesign is requested.
3. **Decision levels:** Timestamp/tag eligibility acts on records. The group audit accepts or rejects a whole beta candidate and does not remove records from `E`. Keep these distinct from held-out candidate selection.
4. **Reference identity:** Explain the preset linear source-multiplier rule versus the exponential pipeline with fixed beta. Similar names or absence of search do not make their weight constructions identical.
5. **Operational accessibility:** Put the defining operations in ordinary prose in the main text. An appendix may hold concrete coefficients, candidate values, and selection details; it cannot substitute for the definition.
6. **Preservation and independent judgment:** Retain the valid P1 and correct supplied algorithm. Correct the current mismatches despite the earlier PASS. Do not fabricate an experiment, alter the inputs/constants, or claim to have run code or inspected files beyond the fixture.

Different wording and reasonable sentence grouping are acceptable. A response that quotes a defective sentence in its explanation has not necessarily endorsed it.
