Archived supervision and editor feedback overlap: uncertainty-guided annotation extends archive training in [A], while [C] retrains on retained archive cases and later editor corrections. How records contribute during fitting is a separate design choice: [B] weights archived cases by annotator agreement, whereas [C] uses equal example weights. Our framework addresses this design choice by weighting archived cases and later corrections using both source identity and case-level consistency. It selects among fixed weighting settings on development data; [C] uses development data to select the retraining stopping point. The next section defines our weighting and selection procedures.

The cards contradict the mutually exclusive camps and the claim that combining archives with feedback is new. They also show that case-level evidence already informs training weights in [B]. They cannot establish a field-wide research gap, a “first” claim for the present combination, or superiority over these systems.
