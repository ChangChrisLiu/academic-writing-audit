# Prespecified cross-domain semantic rubric

Judge each supplied case on six dimensions using pass, qualified, or fail and concrete output evidence. Fictional source facts define the task. Do not compare against a fixed wording or use an AI-authorship score.

1. Concept alignment. Correct or identify category mistakes, misnamed quantities, false properties, scope changes, and implications unsupported by the source. Do not merely soften the same wrong proposition.
2. Specialist-term preservation. Keep legitimate technical terms, disciplinary uses, and author-defined concepts. Preserve the correct opening passage in each case when returning a revision; in economics explicitly identify the sound first paragraph as a keep decision. Retain the spectroscopy caption and the qualitative quotation. Necessary inflection or formatting is not a semantic error, but unexplained restyling of correct source should be noted.
3. Semantic invariants. Preserve values, units, thresholds, populations, conditions, negation, distinct concepts, and supplied operational definitions. Do not broaden or narrow a whole/subset relation or promote association or qualitative accounts into a causal or population claim.
4. Unknowns and evidence. Keep undefined trace capacity, adaptive resilience, welfare resilience, or institutional elasticity unresolved. Do not assert a missing analysis was never done. A definition of a new construct is not validation of a claimed effect.
5. Language repair. Replace inflated labels and unnatural combinations with a clear supported operation or observation. Retain the valid concept while repairing grammar or agency. Do not replace one vague label with another or dumb down all field language.
6. Task and deliverable boundary. Respect revision versus audit-only versus actual isolated editing. Keep author questions and edit-history language out of manuscript prose. Use the requested prose format and preserve notation/quotes. Claims about file changes must match saved files.

A no-change decision on a correct technical passage is a successful outcome. These guided development cases cannot establish universal effectiveness or a causal improvement over no skill.
