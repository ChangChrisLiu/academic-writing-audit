## Revised passage

Absorption spectra were measured in phosphate buffer at pH 7.4. The absorption maximum shifted from 410 nm to 435 nm, corresponding to a bathochromic shift of 25 nm. We define spectral persistence as the fraction of the initial absorbance remaining after 30 minutes under this protocol.

The spectral persistence was 0.82.

## Reasons for material edits

The phrase “performs a 25 nm enhancement on quantum yield” conflates the measured change in the absorption maximum with a different quantity. Quantum yield is not measured in nanometres, and no quantum-yield result or fluorescence measurement was supplied. The supported observation is the 25 nm bathochromic shift already reported in the first paragraph, so the erroneous clause was removed without repeating that result. The wording “performs an enhancement on” is also unnatural, but changing the verb alone would leave the quantity error intact.

The phrase “thereby validating the charge-transfer mechanism” was removed because the supplied absorption observations do not identify that mechanism. This is a correction to the scientific claim. It does not establish whether other supporting analyses exist elsewhere.

“Bathochromic shift” correctly describes the shift toward longer wavelength, and “phosphate buffer” correctly names the chemical solution. Both were retained. “Spectral persistence” was retained with the author's operational definition and reported value of 0.82. The first paragraph, the sentence reporting spectral persistence, and the caption were otherwise sound and remain unchanged.

## Unresolved meaning

The sentence “These findings engender a superior adaptive resilience” was omitted because the meaning and measurement of adaptive resilience are not supplied. Its definition, measured result, and comparator for “superior” remain unresolved. The supplied observations also do not establish the causal relationship implied by “engender.” A replacement requires the author's intended construct and supporting evidence. Spectral persistence cannot be substituted for adaptive resilience without that clarification.
