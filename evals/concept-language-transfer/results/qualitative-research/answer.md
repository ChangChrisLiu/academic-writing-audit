## Reasons for the revision

The final paragraph exceeded what the supplied interview evidence supports. I replaced it with a descriptive interpretation of participants' accounts and a statement of the study's inferential scope. The nine accounts of adjustments, five accounts of rules leaving no room, and four interviews that did not discuss the issue describe a purposive sample of 18 interviews at one workplace. They do not identify a causal effect of quiet agency or estimate the proportion of the national workforce with flexible scheduling. The replacement retains both the reported discretion and the accounts of constraint without treating silence from four participants as either agreement or disagreement.

I removed the assertion of complete saturation because no saturation assessment is supplied and whether one exists is unknown. This omission does not establish that saturation was absent or that no assessment occurred. I did not substitute a different methodological procedure or invent a saturation finding.

I removed the sentence about an emic method executing a generalization enhancement because it names no intelligible analytical operation and supplies no basis for broader inference. Emic meanings remain defined as participants' own categories. I also removed the assertion that institutional elasticity was demonstrated because the supplied material gives neither a definition nor evidence connecting the accounts to that construct. Its intended meaning remains an author question rather than a guessed replacement.

The first two paragraphs remain unchanged. Thick description and emic meanings are legitimate methodological terms with appropriate supplied meanings. Quiet agency is an explicitly defined analytical category, so its name and definition are retained. The participant counts and their distinct meanings are preserved, as is the exact quotation “We learned to bend time.” The quotation remains a participant's expression rather than being converted into a literal mechanism or evidence of causation. No reviewer reply was added to the manuscript.

## Author questions

Was saturation assessed? If so, what form of saturation was assessed, by what procedure, and what documented finding would justify reporting it here? The supplied material does not establish whether such an assessment exists.

What does institutional elasticity mean in this study, and which analytical observations would support that interpretation? Please clarify how it differs from the defined category of quiet agency before reintroducing the claim.

Did the phrase about an emic method and generalization enhancement refer to a specific analytical operation or a particular form of qualitative generalization? If so, please identify the operation and its evidential basis. Neither is recoverable from the supplied passage.
