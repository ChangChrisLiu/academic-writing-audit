## Analysis

We use thick description to situate participants' accounts in their social context. Emic meanings refer to participants' own categories. We define quiet agency as participants' accounts of making small discretionary scheduling adjustments within formal rules.

Nine of the 18 participants described these adjustments, five said formal rules left no room for them, and four did not discuss the issue. One participant said “We learned to bend time.”

These accounts illustrate both perceived room for discretionary scheduling adjustments and perceived constraints within formal rules at one workplace. The purposive interview sample does not establish a causal effect or national prevalence.
