## Genuine defects versus optional choices

The original drafts contain real defects. Mathematics confuses a null set with an empty set and derives a false supremum. Chemistry assigns wavelength units to quantum yield and claims an unidentified mechanism. Economics replaces a household-spending association with a market-share causal claim and overstates fixed-effects identification. Qualitative research promotes a workplace interview account into causality and national prevalence. Each output repairs or diagnoses these propositions rather than only weakening their tone.

By contrast, leaving the correct opening intact is a successful editorial decision. Keeping unusual author-defined names is appropriate because the supplied definitions govern their meaning. Deleting unsupported clauses is acceptable when all supported observations already remain. Heading labels in the revision answers and a short retained chemistry paragraph are not, by themselves, violations of the requested connected-prose format. None of these optional presentation choices is scored as a defect.

The undefined constructs stay unresolved in every case. Requests for their definitions are paired with requests for relevant evidence or comparison, so the answers do not imply that naming or defining a construct establishes an improvement or effect. Missing supplied analyses are not described as analyses that were never performed.
