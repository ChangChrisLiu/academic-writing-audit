# Independent assessment

## Scope and overall finding

This assessment compares the six guided task units in `runs/prose.md` and `runs/transfer.md`, and the four no-repository-skill control units in `runs/control.md`, with the supplied case packets and frozen rubric. It evaluates the passages and audit responses, not an authorship score or their length alone.

**No material failure is identifiable in any of the ten units.** All required numerical results, scientific qualifications, provenance, and distinctions between manuscript and response-letter readers are preserved where applicable. The principal optional refinements concern the ordering of A1 and the narrative focus of B2. Neither warrants treating a scientifically faithful output as a failure.

This finding is limited to the supplied fictional facts and written outputs. It does not verify a theorem, underlying study, native document, rendered artifact, or external source.

## Descriptive comparison and uncertainty

For the four matched prose tasks, both guided and control outputs satisfy the supplied scientific and editorial requirements. A2 is unchanged in both. Both A3 audits identify the consequential errors, with the guided version naming the almost-sure boundary more explicitly. Guided A4 combines the measures and rationale into one sentence before the quotation; control A4 separates them. Both forms are adequate. Guided A1 places implementation first and its reliability explanation outside the passage; control A1 leads with the ablations and retains the reliability boundary within the passage. These are observable choices, not evidence that one output is uniformly better.

There is no control counterpart for B1 or B2 in the supplied runs. Author preference between otherwise sound formulations, effects on a complete manuscript's flow, and robustness across repeated tasks cannot be determined from these packets. The common runtime instructions and these small, unreplicated tasks do not establish a causal or general skill effect. The observed result is narrower: both conditions produced acceptable responses on the four matched tasks, and the two additional guided transfer units also meet their substantive requirements, with the optional abstract-focus refinement described above.
