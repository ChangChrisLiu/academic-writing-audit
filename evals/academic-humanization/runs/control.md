## A1

Three separate ablations on the same held-out archive evaluated timing, calibration and storage, each with a different comparator configuration. The timing ablation reduced median detection delay from 6.0 to 4.5 seconds at equal observation count. The calibration ablation reduced mean absolute error from 0.8 to 0.6 volts. The storage ablation reduced peak memory from 48 to 32 MB. The implementation maintains a replay buffer containing recent transitions. A robust M-estimator limits the influence of outliers in the calibration ablation, and a gate controls hidden-state updates in the timing model. No single configuration demonstrated all three gains simultaneously, and these archive results do not establish deployment reliability.

## A3

The proposed wording does not preserve meaning. It turns the unconstrained ranking score z into a probability of correctness, which the definition does not support. It also removes the specified mode of convergence. The theorem establishes convergence in probability under the stated assumptions, and the proposed sentence omits both that qualification and those assumptions. It provides no basis for a stronger convergence claim. Finally, the proposal changes the required notation f: X → Y and the required range 10–15.
