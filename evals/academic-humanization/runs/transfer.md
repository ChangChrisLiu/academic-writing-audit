## B2

We study detection of changes in a dilute solution's composition from intermittent sensor readings, with sampling intervals that vary because measurements consume power. The time-agnostic baseline used in this study discards these intervals. Our method represents elapsed time alongside the sensor readings. Calibration uses a chemical buffer of fixed pH. In a held-out retrospective evaluation of 64 recorded change episodes, median detection delay was 7.1 seconds for our method versus 9.0 seconds for the time-agnostic baseline at equal mean measurement count.
