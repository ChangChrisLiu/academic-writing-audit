# Frozen rubric

Assess scientific preservation and identifiable prose defects, not an authorship score or exact preferred phrasing. Shorter is not automatically better. A no-change decision may pass. Distinguish a material failure, optional refinement, and a supported choice.

A1 must retain all three comparisons, units and equal-count condition, while keeping separate ablations distinct from a joint result. Do not delete a real third endpoint to avoid a triad. Remove or flag unsupported reliability and promotional claims. Do not rename a genuine replay buffer, robust M-estimator or gate simply because of a word list. Technical implementation details may be omitted from the Results paragraph if their scientific role and reporting scope are preserved in a brief note; do not misstate them. No invented significance or deployment facts.

A2 is a supported clear paragraph. Preserve the association, interval spanning decrease and increase, and lack of causal identification. Do not convert the passive association into an active causal claim or delete uncertainty as timid prose. Retaining the entire paragraph is the preferred no-change behavior, but judge any edit by its actual consequence rather than literal identity alone.

A3 is audit-only. Identify the uncalibrated-score/probability error, lost probabilistic mode and assumptions, and changed required notation. Do not silently repair either source or report a theorem verification. Meaningful scientific contrast and mathematical punctuation are not prose defects.

A4 should expose the supplied rationale for using both measures, remove chat residue and empty praise, keep the exact quotation including colon and dash, and avoid inventing feelings, failure prediction, design history or facts from the unrelated style sample. The final passage need not imitate the sample's sentence count or use rhetorical contrast.

B1 must answer selection and scope with the completed Section 2 and Section 4 locations. A salutation and courteous acknowledgement may remain. Manuscript prose must retain archival provenance, 42 eligible notices, the eligibility rule, two-coder independent coding and reconciliation, and the single-city/no-behavior scope. Reviewer address and revision status belong in the response, not the manuscript. Do not claim that no behavioral study was ever done merely because outcomes are not supplied. No unsolicited extra draft, city, interview or experiment.

B2 should normally fit six sentences, explain the power/interval problem and scientific move, and end with the actual supported principal result, including retrospective held-out scope, comparator and matched measurement budget. No global absence claim, invented safety benefit, generic final slogan or module inventory. Chemical buffer is a legitimate term and may be omitted as secondary abstract detail without being mislabeled as a dataset.

Across cases, preserve author register without manufactured personality, meaningful contrast and repetitions serving different reader purposes. Follow explicit prose-punctuation preferences while preserving technical syntax and exact quotations. Retain necessary research-process statements and source provenance. Never claim a native document, rendered artifact or external evidence was checked. Agents see the packets and their assigned skills, not this rubric or one another's output. The control sees only the same prose packet. Directory restrictions are prompt-based. These small fictional trials are development evidence, not proof of general effectiveness or causal improvement.
