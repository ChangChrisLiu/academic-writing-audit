## Methods

Each procedure used four panels with five indentation readings per panel. Indentation readings were averaged within each panel. The archived annotation states “Eligibility: both records — never infer a missing record.”

## Results

Panels from both procedures were tested after temperature stabilization. Only panels with both thickness and temperature records were included, and each panel was the independent unit. The cross-batch panel means were 6.8, 7.0, 7.2, and 7.4 GPa, and the fixed-baseline panel means were 5.8, 6.0, 6.2, and 6.4 GPa. The averages across the four independent panels per procedure were 7.1 and 6.1 GPa, respectively, an observed difference of 1.0 GPa. The procedures differed in both weighting and coating thickness, so this comparison does not isolate the contribution of weighting.
