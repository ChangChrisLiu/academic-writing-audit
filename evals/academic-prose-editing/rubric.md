# Prespecified semantic checks

These checks concern concrete editing behavior. They do not score AI authorship or demand a single reference wording. Evaluate the supplied material only. Record pass, qualified, or fail with output evidence for each applicable dimension.

1. Task scope and preservation. Keep audit-only work as an audit. Edit only authorized source regions and preserve sound motivation, protected content, numerical values, independent units, eligibility, and conditions. In the paired case verify letter assertions against the actual edited file.
2. Logic. Identify or repair unsupported attribution and endpoint changes, vague referents, and missing premises. Do not upgrade confidence through stronger stylistic verbs. Missing input must not become a study-absence fact.
3. Readability and natural expression. Reduce obscuring clause nesting and empty staging without losing connections or turning all prose into short fragments. Identify sentence-complexity problems explicitly in the audit-only case. Keep terminology stable and avoid canned replacement prose.
4. Redundancy. Remove or identify repeated propositions and empty summaries. Preserve useful motivation and legitimate standalone summaries or captions even when facts recur.
5. Prose format and exceptions. In generated explanatory prose, avoid colons, sentence-interrupting dashes, and bullet fragments. Preserve technical compound hyphens, formal notation, minus signs, ranges, ratios, URLs, quotations, and required syntax. Inspect occurrences in context, not by punctuation count alone.
6. Response/manuscript boundary where applicable. Reviewer IDs and revision talk stay in the response. The paper directly explains the study. Pending work is not completed work. Unknown analysis status remains an author query or explicit pending response status.

A pass applies to the inspected case, not the entire library or all future writing. Report unexecuted paths and residual uncertainty.
