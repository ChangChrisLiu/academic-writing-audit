# Comparing revisions without rewarding verbosity

The comparison unit is a scientific proposition, a dependency between propositions, or a reader function. It need not be one sentence. Several sentences may compress safely into one, while a single omitted condition may invalidate a conclusion.

## A proportionate working map

| Question | What to establish |
| --- | --- |
| What was the revision meant to fix? | The actual request or concern, distinct from an inferred reader expectation. |
| What must remain understandable? | The local question, scientific objects, defining operation, and warranted inference. |
| What changed? | Meaning, scope, logical connection, location, and reader access, including additions. |
| Is the change justified? | Evidence supporting a correction or relocation, not preference for either version. |
| What is the smallest remaining action? | Keep the improvement, restore a missing fact, repair a link, or identify a decisive unknown. |

For a full section, check its opening question, dependency order, and ending answer. For a paragraph, read its neighbors. For a local wording edit, do not demand the entire manuscript unless the decision genuinely depends on it.

## Fictional contrasts

**Experimental endpoint.** The source defines success as reaching a target and maintaining a safe grip for five seconds, over all scheduled trials including aborted ones. Replacing that definition with “target-reaching success” changes both the endpoint and potentially the denominator. Restore the missing operation and denominator in compact prose. Removing “we emphasize the rigor of this criterion” is useful compression.

**Motivation.** A method combines routine observations with follow-up observations because the latter sample unusual conditions. Deleting that distinction may leave every method component named while removing the reason for combining them. Retain a short explanation of their different roles. Do not claim that each role independently causes the observed gain without the relevant evidence.

**Theory.** A theorem valid for each fixed parameter on a compact domain is shortened to a uniform guarantee over all parameters on an unrestricted domain. Stable notation and unchanged conclusions do not preserve the theorem. Restore the actual assumptions and quantifier order. If the old prose claimed uniqueness but the supplied proof establishes existence only, accept a supported correction to existence rather than restoring uniqueness.

**Interpretive research.** Removing a discordant case can convert a situated interpretation into a unanimous or population-wide claim. Preserve the countercase's analytic role without requiring participant counts, inter-rater reliability, or saturation claims that the study's approach does not support. Conversely, repeated descriptions of the same coding step may merge safely.

**Relocation.** Moving a long parameter table to an inspected appendix with an accurate reference can improve the main text. Moving the sole definition of the outcome behind a generic “details elsewhere” pointer leaves the main result uninterpretable. If an appendix is unavailable, distinguish an unverified move from confirmed deletion.

**Response letter.** “We have clarified eligibility” may accurately describe an edit while the new paragraph still fails to identify eligible records. Verify the operation, not the response's confidence. A clear revised paragraph can resolve a concern without repeating the reviewer's question in the paper.

## Resolving conflicting judgments

If a reviewer asks to restore detail and an editor calls it redundant, identify the fact's reader function and location. Retain it when it changes interpretation or supplies a missing dependency; shorten or relocate it when another inspected passage already performs that function adequately. Explain the source-based decision. Neither role has an automatic veto, and majority agreement is not evidence.

Report remaining uncertainty precisely. “The supplied excerpt does not show the denominator” differs from “the authors excluded failed trials.” A verified local repair differs from confirmation that the entire response letter, final PDF, or submission package is correct.
