# Prespecified manuscript-structure trial rubric

Assess the complete outputs against each supplied fictional packet, not stylistic phrase matching. Use pass, qualified, or fail per applicable dimension with exact anchors. State N/A when a dimension is not exercised. A partial draft is correct when necessary research facts are absent; do not reward fabricated completeness.

1. Research grounding. Understand the particular question and available evidence before writing. No generic invented gap, novelty, sources, results, or imported facts; missing decisive facts are specific dependencies.
2. Argument progression. Abstract and introduction preserve a motivated question-to-contribution-to-evidence progression when requested. Introduction narrows through relevant progress, gap, motivation, and contribution. Do not force empirical structure on the mathematical or interpretive tasks.
3. Literature synthesis. The review follows connected threads motivated by the introduction, explains prior work accurately, and avoids artificial camps, a paper-by-paper catalogue, or default self-comparison. Citation placement and uncertainty match source coverage.
4. Methods, findings, and ending. Where requested, method follows real dependencies without repeated assurances; the findings and ending suit the design. Preserve quantities, assumptions, uncertainty, countercases, null/untested status, and the supplied section division. Do not turn themes into frequencies or an estimate into an isolated effect.
5. Concision and reader voice. Connected substantive prose, no obligatory contribution list or module checklist, repeated defenses, generic significance claims, or reviewer-facing manuscript language. Retain technical terms, material conditions, and logical bridges.
6. Format and delivery honesty. Respect LaTeX commands and keys or paste-ready Word conventions; preserve technical punctuation. Check observable source structure, without claiming compilation, actual .docx generation, or rendered layout from source inspection.

Evaluate actual artifacts and identify concrete harmful or material errors separately from optional editorial preferences. No unguided control or causal-effect claim is planned. Three fictional cases on the same configured host do not establish universal reliability or full document-format preservation.
