The exact-iteration geometric estimate and the perturbed-iteration upper bound therefore describe distinct conclusions under their respective assumptions \cite{c1,c2}.

The strict contraction condition also separates these estimates from results for nonexpansive mappings. The nonexpansive exposition discusses additional conditions for particular iteration schemes, so its conclusions must be read at the level of those schemes rather than inferred from nonexpansiveness alone \cite{c3}.
