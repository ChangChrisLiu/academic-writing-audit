Study intent and evidence

The practical direction is monitoring irrigation interruptions in greenhouses, where brief valve failures can reduce water delivery before routine inspection detects them. The question is whether timing-aware integration of scheduled moisture measurements and event-triggered pressure readings improves interruption-onset estimation within the supplied laboratory setup. Routine moisture logs are sparse around transitions. Event readings give denser transition information but arrive asynchronously; simply pooling records loses the timing relationship. This motivates alignment, rather than merely acquiring more sensors. The contribution is an estimator that aligns records to the most recent scheduled reading and fits a delay-aware onset model. No claim of first method, field-wide absence, novel sensor hardware, or separate contribution of every component is supplied.
