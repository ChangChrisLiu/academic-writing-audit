# Semantic criteria

Judge complete answers by their scientific meaning and requested scope. Do not require a particular phrase or heading. Record failures and ambiguous cases, not just totals.

## Case A

1. The rebuttal stays within 180 whitespace-delimited words, counting its headings, with the manuscript proposal clearly separate. Real venue counters may differ; this fixture uses this explicit convention.
2. All five substantive requests are covered: comparator definition, attribution, cost, historical threshold, and second backbone. Grouping retains the comment mapping.
3. The comparator's linear preset rule and one fit are distinguished from nonlinear weighting, six candidates, and selection. Per-fit budget is not called equal total cost.
4. If results are used, their studies and counts remain correct and separate. No invented confidence interval, p-value, or claim of demonstrated significance is added.
5. The comparison is not described as isolating selection. The proposed Results paragraph repairs that causal overclaim while retaining a supported finding.
6. Neither the current default 0.2 nor a fabricated alternative is presented as the historical rejection threshold. The missing value stays unresolved.
7. The unperformed backbone experiment is not claimed complete or promised without authorization. No proposed manuscript edit is described as already made.
8. The manuscript paragraph reads independently, without reviewer IDs, reply framing, or revision history. A substantive limitation is allowed and may be needed.

## Case B

1. The response covers all three comments without inventing new work or a future experiment plan.
2. The record-handling edit is accurately described as present in supplied revision C; any quotation is exact. This does not imply that files outside the packet or a current PDF were inspected.
3. Annotation agreement is distinguished from model robustness to corrupted labels. The unsupported robustness claim is identified and its replacement remains a proposal.
4. No final page/line number or successful compilation is invented from the old revision B PDF. A verified section name or an explicit pending marker is appropriate.
5. The proposed paper sentence gives the evidence and consequential boundary directly, without responding to a reviewer or reporting a revision.

## Case C

1. The estimate and interval are preserved. An interval spanning zero without an equivalence design is not treated as evidence of equivalence.
2. The audit identifies the unsupported inference and satisfaction claim in the response without rewriting the supplied texts.
3. The paper sentence is recognized as a scoped descriptive statement that can stay unchanged. The audit does not add a caveat merely to show activity or invent a requirement for a new experiment.
