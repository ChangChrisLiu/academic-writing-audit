# Independent assessment of six fictional editorial trials

## Scope and method

This assessment uses only the transfer rubric, the six case packets, and the complete saved artifacts for ecology, education, materials, mathematics, history, and peer-review research under `results/v0.3.0/`. References below are relative to `open-source/academic-writing-audit/evals/reviewer-response-writing/transfer/`. No memories, skill instructions, repository READMEs, prior judgments, other workspace material, or network sources were consulted. The execution records were read as trial artifacts; their listed input files were not opened.

I first assessed the proposed or edited manuscript text on its own, then reconciled it with each response and the permitted evidence. I also re-extracted and reread the manuscript-only sections. For materials, I compared the saved manuscript against the original manuscript embedded in the case packet and checked the response's revision quotations. I independently counted the two constrained rebuttals using whitespace delimitation, including their headings. The execution records are self-reported process records, not independent proof of every historical action. The saved deliverables and the comparisons described here support the output-level findings.

The judgments distinguish a research limitation that helps a paper reader interpret the evidence from language that debates or reassures the submission's reviewers. Neither a negation nor the word “reviewer” is sufficient evidence of a failure. A truthful explanation that a requested item cannot be supplied can satisfy response coverage without completing that underlying item.

## Ecology

It neither attributes the limitation to a reviewer nor declares a concern resolved. The adjacent sentence, “The reported means alone do not establish the precision of the difference,” is an informative boundary on the reported result, not a reassurance to the reviewers.

Consequence: A reader can interpret the observed contrast and its limits without seeing the review exchange. The text contains necessary inferential negation but no remaining reviewer-facing exposition.

### 2. Evidence and inference — pass

All supplied counts and means remain intact. The explanation distinguishes a possible selection contribution from an observed or estimated confounding effect. It does not invent matching, adjustment, uncertainty estimates, or baseline observations. The original unsupported recovery claim is removed while the observed regional contrast remains.

Consequence: The supported descriptive finding survives; neither causal recovery nor broader temporal or geographic applicability is asserted.

The introduction explicitly says no files have been edited. Unknown page and line references remain placeholders.

### 4. Scope and deliverable — pass

## Cross-case issues, preferences, and correction decision

No factual scope/status error, over-removal, or remaining reviewer-facing manuscript passage was demonstrated in these six artifacts. The repaired texts preserve the supported contributions: the ecology contrast, mixed teacher experiences, materials adhesion values, mathematical existence theorem, corpus-specific historical practices, and peer-review omission-detection counts.

The following are useful distinctions, not hidden failures:

- The history archival identifiers and quotations remain unavailable. The response explicitly reports the request as incomplete and separates that fact from the already-known corpus-selection rule.
- Informative statements about unavailable baseline measurements, uncertainty, unmeasured outcomes, and geographic scope remain in manuscript text. They explain interpretation rather than merely assuring a reviewer that a concern is handled.
- Some scope statements could be made more concise, especially ecology's “consistent with higher richness” and history's repeated national-inference boundary. The rubric does not require a particular prose style, and these phrases do not introduce a false inference or replace a supported result.
- The peer-review trial retains “reviewers” as a participant label and random assignment in the response and unchanged Methods. A keyword-based purge would damage the study description.
- Materials provides the strongest direct status reconciliation because the current edited manuscript is present. Its diff and all three replacement quotations agree with the response. The other five requests are drafts and consistently remain drafts.

**Smallest general instruction correction justified by a genuine failure: none.** No demonstrated failure or concrete harmful ambiguity in these outputs warrants changing the skill. The current trial artifacts should remain intact. These all-pass guided outputs also do not supply evidence of an improvement over unguided performance, and the limited fictional sample should not be presented as universal validation.
