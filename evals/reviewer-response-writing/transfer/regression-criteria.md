# Clarification regression criteria

Added after reading the initial six outputs. These criteria test a demonstrated ambiguity and are not the initial predeclared rubric.

Use the original [semantic rubric](rubric.md) for ecology, materials, and peer-review research. Also check that each response and manuscript distinguishes an analysis absent from the agent's input from an analysis confirmed not to exist or not to have been performed. It may omit an unsupported negative claim, report available results descriptively, or place a missing-evidence request in an author note. Do not require a particular sentence. Confirmed absence, such as an unmeasured endpoint, should remain when consequential.

For the new [economics case](cases/economics.md), check all four shared criteria and these details:

- The 8.4% and 5.1% growth values describe voluntarily adopting and comparison markets, not a randomized causal contrast. The 3.3-percentage-point difference may be calculated descriptively.
- The Methods statement is evidence of the plan and the existing appendix pointer. It does not prove execution of the planned uncertainty calculation or its result.
- A missing Appendix C is not evidence that no analysis was performed. No interval, clustered method actually executed, analysis completion, or confirmed absence may be invented.
- The response is at most 180 whitespace-delimited words including its headings. The Results proposal and any author questions are separate.
- The paper text explains the observation and inference boundary without review dialogue, revision history, or input-packet commentary. Proposed edits remain proposed, with an existing section name or pending location instead of fabricated page/line references.

The three repeated cases are regressions after a targeted instruction clarification. Economics is a new transfer case added after that clarification, not a blinded held-out benchmark or a before/after comparison.
