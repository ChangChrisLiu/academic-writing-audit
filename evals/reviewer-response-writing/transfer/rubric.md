# Cross-domain semantic rubric

Written before inspecting trial outputs. Each case is a fictional, self-contained editorial request. Judge the full requested deliverables, not keyword counts or resemblance to an example.

## Shared criteria

1. **Manuscript independence:** Proposed or edited paper text explains the research to a reader who has not seen the reviews. No submission-reviewer IDs, thanks, revision chronology, declarations that a concern is resolved, or indirect debate/reassurance should remain in ordinary exposition. Legitimate research terms, necessary negations, and informative limitations are allowed. “Reviewer” is a legitimate participant label in the peer-review-research case.
2. **Evidence and inference:** Preserve supplied results and definitions. Do not invent observations, analyses, citations, uncertainty, quotations, or historical facts. Correct unsupported causal, population, theoretical, or endpoint inferences without deleting the supported contribution.
3. **Coverage and status:** Answer every substantive comment. Distinguish an existing statement, a proposed revision, and an actually completed edit. Do not promise unauthorized work or invent page/line references or render verification.
4. **Scope and deliverable:** Produce the requested response format and separate manuscript material. Respect word limits, permitted evidence, language, and edit scope. Preserve unaffected or explicitly approved passages. A warranted no-change decision is valid.

Mark each criterion pass, fail, or qualified, with an exact supporting excerpt and the consequence of any issue. Do not hide a failure behind an overall score. Formatting variation alone is not a failure.

## Case-specific checks

| Case | Necessary distinctions |
| --- | --- |
| Ecology | Observed regional, one-season contrast versus causal recovery; no random assignment or pre-restoration measurements; existing Methods statement not newly added; page/lines unavailable; no invented matching, adjustment, or fieldwork. |
| Education | Purposive interviews about experience versus nationwide effectiveness/student outcomes; include the four increased-workload accounts; term-end recruitment is not demonstrated saturation; no invented interview quotes; rebuttal at most 150 whitespace-delimited words, headings included. |
| Materials | Three independent panels per formulation, five nested indents each; six panel-mean values preserved; thickness and formulation differ together; no fabricated inferential analysis; Methods and Conclusion unchanged; actual file edit matches response letter. |
| Mathematics | Nonempty compact domain and continuity imply existence; uniqueness removed; compact image contains its infimum; no unwanted convexity condition, application change, or empirical experiment; corrected text remains proposed. |
| History | Surviving, catalogued letters from two archives versus national prevalence; sampling/survival limits and 26/38 count remain interpretable; no invented accession IDs or quotations; existing Appendix A location accurate; Chinese outputs and no unapproved archive visit. |
| Peer-review research | Retain reviewer participants and random assignment; counts 51/85 and 44/85; measured omission detection in one abstract, not publication quality or full-manuscript outcomes; no invented significance or new data; rebuttal at most 160 whitespace-delimited words, headings included. |

## Review and repair policy

Read the full manuscript output and response separately, then reconcile them. A phrase search may locate candidate issues but cannot determine whether a limitation is useful or a research term legitimate. Independently review disputed judgments against the case packet.

Repair a skill only for a demonstrated decision failure or a concrete ambiguity exposed by the output. Retain the failing response and its original skill version. Re-run affected cases in fresh contexts after a change; label repeated development cases as regressions, not new held-out evidence. A small same-host sample cannot establish universal or cross-model generality, and guided-only runs cannot establish improvement over an unguided baseline.
