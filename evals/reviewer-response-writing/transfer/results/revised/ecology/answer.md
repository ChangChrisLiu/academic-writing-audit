**R1.1: Is the richness difference evidence that restoration caused a recovery? Please clarify selection and baseline conditions.**

**Response:** The comparison shows higher observed mean richness at the restored sites, but it does not establish a causal effect of restoration or a recovery trajectory. Mean observed richness was 18.2 species at 14 previously restored sites and 14.7 species at 14 reference sites. These sites were selected from an existing regional registry, and restoration status was not randomly assigned. There are no pre-restoration richness measurements, so the comparison cannot establish whether richness increased after restoration or whether the two groups had comparable baseline richness. Pre-existing site differences or other factors could contribute to the observed difference. The original causal statement and assurance that the study was “certainly not biased” are therefore not warranted.
