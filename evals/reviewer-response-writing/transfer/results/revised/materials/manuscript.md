# Adhesion study

## Methods
Three independently prepared panels were measured for each formulation. Five indents were sampled within each panel. The enhanced formulation used a 40-micrometre coating; the reference formulation used a 20-micrometre coating.

## Results
The panel-mean adhesion values were 7.1, 7.5, and 7.3 MPa for the enhanced formulation and 5.8, 6.2, and 6.0 MPa for the reference formulation. The independently prepared unit was the panel (n = 3 per formulation); the five indents within each panel were subsamples. The mean of the three panel means was 7.3 MPa for the enhanced formulation and 6.0 MPa for the reference formulation, an observed difference of 1.3 MPa. Because the formulations also differed in coating thickness, this contrast does not isolate the contribution of additive X.

## Conclusion
The observed formulation contrast motivates further study of adhesion under matched coating thickness.
