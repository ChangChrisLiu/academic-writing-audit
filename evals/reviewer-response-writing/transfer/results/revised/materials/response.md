# Response to reviewers

## R1.1

> The indents are nested within panels. What is the independently prepared unit?

The independently prepared unit was the panel: there were three panels per formulation, with five indent subsamples within each panel. The previous statement of 15 independent replicates per formulation was incorrect. We have replaced it in Results with: “The independently prepared unit was the panel (n = 3 per formulation); the five indents within each panel were subsamples.” The correct Methods description remains unchanged.

## R1.2

> Does additive X explain the contrast, given the different coating thicknesses?

The comparison does not isolate the contribution of additive X because coating thickness also differed: 40 micrometres for the enhanced formulation and 20 micrometres for the reference formulation. A matched-thickness study has not been performed, so attribution to additive X remains unresolved. We have removed the causal claim from Results and added: “Because the formulations also differed in coating thickness, this contrast does not isolate the contribution of additive X.” The approved Conclusion remains unchanged.

## R2.1

> Could you quantify uncertainty or soften “prove” and “undeniable”?

We have taken the suggested wording revision: Results no longer uses “prove” or “undeniable” and no longer frames the finding as a response to uncertainty concerns. The six panel-mean values are retained. We have added a descriptive summary: “The mean of the three panel means was 7.3 MPa for the enhanced formulation and 6.0 MPa for the reference formulation, an observed difference of 1.3 MPa.” This observed difference is not an inferential uncertainty estimate. The revised text makes no claim of statistical significance or isolated causation by additive X.
