# Frozen assessment rubric

Assess the full outputs against supplied evidence, not preferred wording. A passing output may preserve the source verbatim. Report material failures separately from minor editorial opportunities. Mark a criterion not applicable when the packet does not invoke it.

Engineering abstract should normally have at most six real sentences, with a necessary task-to-difficulty-to-scientific-move bridge and a final principal supported result. It must not hide an inventory in long semicolon chains. Detection delay, equal measurement budget, the time-agnostic comparator and retrospective held-out scope must remain accurate. The mechanism should explain the contribution without replacing the problem with a list of modules. No invented significance, deployment or causal evidence. The prose must not contain reviewer address, reassurance or a generic final impact slogan.

Engineering introduction should have one opening-to-question line, explain the relevant sampling condition and consequence, distinguish the supervised archive route from the reward-dependent exploratory route, and preserve the necessary premise for the time-aware move. No global requirement for rewards or interaction, fixed disadvantage list, invented literature absence or repeated obstacle loop. Dataset and actual replay buffer must not be conflated. A proper compound hyphen or technical command is not a style failure.

Literature A should develop observation availability, timing interpretation and relevant evidence in coherent prose rather than preset bins, one paragraph per paper or disguised family listing. Do not invent extensions, historical dependence or a common trained-model mechanism. Keep a dataset, a sensing system and estimators distinct. Do not transfer amplitude error evidence to onset timing or claim an excerpt establishes absence of a capability. No default current-paper superiority comparison. Citations must stay with supported propositions and preserve supplied LaTeX syntax. The order of studies is not prescribed.

Literature B is already coherent and supported. Preserve it verbatim unless a concrete consequential issue can be shown; do not add taxonomy, causal language, population estimates, mandatory contrasts or gratuitous rhythm edits. A plain-text answer must not claim native Word field or rendering verification.

Theoretical and qualitative abstracts should normally be within six sentences and end in the supported theorem or interpretation, retaining scope and important assumptions. Do not impose an empirical score or erase the theoretical conditions. Qualitative interpretation must stay within the interviews and accommodate the supplied exception without requiring a list of all accounts.

Results-pending output must be explicitly provisional, with missing results separated from manuscript prose, and no fabricated finding, finished experiment or automatic refusal to help. A rationale or outline is acceptable. It must not pretend to complete the result-ended arc.

Mandatory-format abstract must follow the seven-sentence author/venue requirement and structured headings. The six-sentence default must not override it. Preserve the observational association, study scale and relevant confounding; no invented statistics or causal effect.

Across all cases, connected writing must stay evidence-bound, concise and readable. Necessary scientific qualifications are not defensive prose. No arbitrary synonym replacement, explanatory colons or parenthetical sentence-interrupting dashes in drafted ordinary prose; mandated headings, citation syntax, technical notation and lexical hyphens are exceptions. A count alone does not establish narrative quality.

A no-guidance control is descriptive only. One unreplicated task does not establish a causal benefit or broad transfer. Restricted reads and separate directories are prompt instructions, not a security boundary. These trials do not exercise real reference retrieval, full-document layout, compilation or native citation fields.
