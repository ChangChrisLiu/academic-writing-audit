## Disagreements, apparent conflicts and unresolved claims

I found no material source-supported defect or actual disagreement requiring correction in A's record. This judgment rests on the cited packet locations, not our agreement. There is no objection quota.

The central contradictions are between the authors' priority pitches and existing work. They are not contradictions among the prior studies.
