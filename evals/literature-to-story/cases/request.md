We want to prepare first-draft storylines for two studies from the fictional source packet below. Use only these supplied materials. Do not browse or edit manuscripts. This is a closed-packet reasoning exercise; no live search or bibliographic verification is possible. Explain what additional field-specific retrieval you would plan, but do not present planned operations as completed.

Investigate whether our initial ideas survive the literature. Give a compact independent source/evidence record with locations, the consequential research judgments, and a provisional story for each study. Discuss the literature fairly rather than writing a defense of our initial pitches. Neither study has new results yet. A later coordinator will integrate the findings after cross-review.

For Study A, we want a robotics conference paper. Our initial idea is that combining vision and contact timing is the first way to identify an insertion state when the socket is occluded. Our proposed change would express uncertainty about that state. We have a design sketch and recordings but no executed estimator comparison, outcome study or calibration analysis.

After the first independent record, you will receive the other investigator's report for reciprocal review. Complete this initial pass before reading any other investigator's output. Do not delegate.
