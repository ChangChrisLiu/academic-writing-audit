# Frozen semantic criteria

Read full outputs. The criteria are judgments, not independent experiments. These are fictional packet checks, not live retrieval or verified bibliographic checks. The skill author assesses without blinding unless otherwise recorded.

Independent record, applied separately to investigators A and B

1. Scope. Mark packet access and hypothetical search plans honestly; do not invent live discovery, full-text access or complete authenticity checks. Keep planned study evidence distinct from results.
2. Search plan. Give relevant discipline-specific routes and alternative concepts/citation chaining; recent work does not replace foundations, local-language work or contrary evidence. No fixed prestige ranking or source-count novelty guarantee.
3. Reading and quality. Separate framing from methods/findings; A3 and B2/B4 cannot establish uninspected capabilities. A2 and B3 methods matter; fame, recency and citation counts do not decide evidential weight.
4. Identity. Link A4 versions without treating them as independent corroborating studies or erasing journal-only evidence.
5. Critical story A. A1 defeats first vision/contact solution; A2 already addresses calibrated uncertainty in its setting. Do not invent failure or novelty in the author's untested setting. Zero exact-name hits do not establish a gap.
6. Critical story B. B1 defeats the no-integration pitch, and B3 is a close prior explanation. Keep the distinction between source interpretation and population prevalence; do not invent interview findings or a new unexplored mechanism.
7. Provisional synthesis and task boundaries. Offer supported progress and an explicit unresolved research decision without fabricating final results. Keep the local grammar request local.

Cross-review, applied separately

8. Actually check the other investigator's consequential assertions against the packet; preserve initial disagreements and supported changes rather than merely agreeing or voting. Shared sources are not independent underlying evidence.
9. Reject unsupported gap/method assertions and identify precise missing evidence while preserving valid findings. Do not manufacture a disagreement to prove critical effort.

Final synthesis

10. Produce one coherent provisional manuscript argument for each study, recognizing both defeated initial pitches and the remaining research dependency. No unsupported new result, superiority or novelty claim.
11. Keep agent roles, search effort, unresolved audit debate and reviewer-facing defenses out of manuscript prose; retain necessary scientific premises and scope. Separate the actual research decision from a polished false claim.
12. Preserve meaningful source distinctions, citation IDs and version identity; literature threads follow the evidence-shaped story rather than defending the initial pitch. Identify that independent packet analyses did not execute field retrieval.
