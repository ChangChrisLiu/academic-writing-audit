# Findings, limitations, and conclusion

## Choose the form from the study

Identify the research question, discipline, article type, supplied reporting requirements, and whether Results and Discussion are separate or combined. Prefer the actual template and supplied field exemplars over broad stereotypes. A discipline can contain several study designs. Use the following as reasoning options, not required headings or reporting-standard claims.

| Study design | Useful progression | Meaning to protect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quantitative experiment or observational study | Primary question and estimate, uncertainty and relevant conditions, then secondary or sensitivity findings that change interpretation. | Unit, denominator, comparison, confounding, null and mixed findings; association is not automatically causation. |
| Engineering or computational evaluation | Task and protocol, principal comparison, then targeted diagnostic or ablation evidence addressing a specific explanation. | Separate benchmarks, shifts, search budgets, support sets, and full-system gains from isolated component effects. |
| Qualitative or interpretive study | Analytic claim or theme, supporting material, interpretation, and variation or a counterexample where supplied. | Context and analytic position; themes are not population frequencies. Do not fabricate quotations, saturation, coder agreement, or validation procedures. |
| Theoretical or mathematical study | Assumptions and definitions needed for the result, proposition, proof dependencies, then a consequence or boundary. | Local versus global statements, existence versus uniqueness, necessary versus sufficient conditions, and proved versus conjectured claims. |
| Mixed-method study | Explain where the evidence streams converge, differ, or answer complementary parts of the question. | An unresolved disagreement cannot be erased by reporting only the convergent stream. |

Do not narrate every table cell, create one paragraph per metric, or manufacture a positive finding to complete the story. Lead with what answers the question. Preserve supported positive findings without adding a disclaimer after every sentence. Put the material condition near the claim it limits. A result table or formal proposition can remain structured when that serves the discipline; surrounding exposition should develop an argument rather than repeat a list.

Respect the journal's supplied division of labor. With a separate Discussion, report the observations and necessary local interpretation in Results, then develop explanations, literature connections, and broader implications in Discussion. With combined Results and Discussion, keep observation and interpretation distinguishable within the passage. Do not invent a reporting-standard requirement or claim compliance without checking the applicable current guidance when that verification is in scope.

## Limitations

Select constraints that affect the answer or its applicability. Explain the constraint, which inference it limits, and the practical or conceptual consequence. A single-site qualitative study, a narrow computational benchmark, and a theorem under a restrictive assumption have different implications; none calls for a stock paragraph about small samples or future deep learning.

Retain a limitation even when it makes the result less general. Missing material in the editor's packet does not prove that the study omitted an analysis. State unresolved evidence separately. Avoid hypothetical objections, repeated defenses, promises of unfinished work, or an automatic shopping list of experiments. Put a limitation where the discipline and manuscript structure make it easiest to interpret, whether in Discussion, a dedicated section, or beside the relevant finding. Do not repeat it in all three places without a distinct reader need.

## Conclusion

Return to the original question and give the supported answer. State the contribution and its bounded significance at the right level, using only the result already established. Do not replay each section, enumerate every component, add a new result, or reopen the entire motivation. Include a future direction only if it follows specifically from a consequential unresolved issue and suits the requested format. A clear ending does not require a generic promise that the work paves the way for future research.
