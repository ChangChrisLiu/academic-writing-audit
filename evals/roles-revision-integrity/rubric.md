# Independent assessment rubric

Assess the exact saved outputs against the raw fictional packets. Score each numbered criterion pass, partial, fail, or not_applicable with an output quotation/location and reason. Do not infer omitted work. Do not read skills or author discussions. Record unexpected harmful changes separately. Return assessment.md plus assessment.json with cases mapping to a list of objects containing criterion, status, evidence, and reason, followed by limitations. Treat optional wording preferences separately from failures. There is no target pass rate.

## Engineering
1. Uses the actual deployment question and experimental design to distinguish scientific review from expression; states expected answer versus present gap rather than only claiming expertise.
2. Detects composite endpoint and denominator corruption, restores the supplied 60-trial basis and meaningful image/timeout condition in any proposal without inventing results.
3. Detects missing motivation and complementary data roles without turning them into isolated causal contributions; rejects optimality and unsupported attribution.
4. Judges concern resolution against actual manuscript operations and evidence, recognizing what the same-data clarification and inspected appendix already supply.
5. Keeps valid buffer coating terminology, accepts removal of reassurance and relocation of detailed calibration constants, and does not restore old text wholesale.
6. Separates manuscript prose from reviewer/status language and does not call proposed repairs completed.
7. Provides a bounded audit and minimal repair grounded in both versions with no unrequested source modification or extra experiments.

## Mathematics
1. Grounds the reviewer/editor lenses and expected logic in the formal claim, assumptions, and parameter dependence, without empirical reporting requirements.
2. Accepts the corrected theorem discussion as scientifically preserved and concisely improved, including the author-approved removal of uniqueness; does not mechanically restore all deleted sentences.
3. Detects the revised abstract's false uniqueness, unrestricted domain, and uniformity; keeps existence distinct from iterative convergence and respects the supplied finite-dimensional scope.
4. Judges the response's accurate local change statement separately from unresolved manuscript-wide consistency and actual reviewer concern resolution.
5. Preserves compact, convex, invariant, and parameter-dependent language as legitimate technical meaning, not awkward prose to flatten.
6. Treats Supplement S2 as unverified relocation, not confirmed loss or confirmed preservation, and scopes the limitation without blocking the available audit.
7. Returns audit only, no fabricated proof, venue rule, new result, or full rewrite; includes justified keep decisions.

## Qualitative
1. Selects the interpretive/reflexive lens by question and study design, making expected argument versus present answer concrete.
2. Detects lost contrary-case reasoning, situated scope, and incorrect unanimity/causal or population-wide implications, not merely a difference in word count.
3. Evaluates reviewer B's request against the retained claim and analytic commitments; does not demand prevalence, coding reliability, saturation, or causal experiments as universal prerequisites.
4. Preserves boundary work and reflexive thematic analysis, accepts the two valid compressions, and does not restore defensive rhetoric wholesale.
5. Proposed manuscript repair restores the relevant research question, setting/sampling and interpretive boundary, and countercase function without unsupported claims or reviewer-facing voice.
6. Proposed reply directly and respectfully explains the mismatch and honest proposed/completed status; does not claim an analysis was conducted or guaranteed reviewer satisfaction.
7. Audits both substantive resolution and information preservation, distinguishing scientific issues from stylistic improvements while keeping output bounded.
