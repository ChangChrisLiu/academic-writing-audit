# Discipline-aware review and revision integrity trials

Three independent-context guided trials check professional reviewing lenses, expected versus current reasoning, and whether manuscript shortening resolves the intended concern without losing scientific meaning. All materials are fictional. This run does not modify or republish the private PACER manuscript, rebuttal, or review correspondence.

## Design and cases

| Case | Requested judgment | Skills exercised |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Robotics inspection](cases/engineering/request.md) | Audit a changed comparator explanation, success definition, denominator, motivation, and reply; propose only local repairs. | academic-writing-audit and revision-integrity-audit |
| [Numerical analysis](cases/mathematics/request.md) | Compare a valid corrected theorem discussion with a strengthened abstract and an unavailable supplement. Audit only. | revision-integrity-audit |
| [Organizational ethnography](cases/qualitative/request.md) | Check contrary accounts, situated interpretation, inappropriate quantitative requests, valid compression, and paired manuscript/reply proposals. | reviewer-response-writing and revision-integrity-audit |

The requests, skill files, and [seven-criterion-per-case rubric](rubric.md) were frozen before execution. Each test agent received its request and assigned skills, with no parent history, rubric, previous outputs, or other cases. Instructions excluded memory and network access. These are task restrictions and saved execution reports, not a claim of enforced filesystem isolation or independently reconstructed tool traces.

A separate fresh-context assessor read the complete packets and exact outputs against the rubric without the skills or parent judgments. The [full assessment](assessment.md) and [structured judgments](assessment.json) are preserved alongside each case's complete answer and execution notes. The public files are byte-identical to the saved trial outputs; they have not been polished after assessment.

## Observed outcomes

The assessor marked all 21 criteria pass, with no partial, failed, or inapplicable judgments, no material disagreement, and no unexpected harmful proposed change. The primary editor also inspected the complete outputs and did not identify a material rule defect requiring a second iteration.

The engineering answer restores the composite inspection endpoint and 60-trial denominator, retains the legitimate buffer-coating term and verified calibration-table relocation, and separates useful motivation from unsupported optimality. The mathematics answer accepts the corrected discussion and removal of unsupported uniqueness while finding the false abstract guarantees. It treats Supplement S2 as unverified, not lost or safely preserved. The qualitative answer retains boundary work and reflexive thematic analysis, restores the analytic role of contrary accounts, accepts two valid compressions, and explains why the requested statistics do not answer this study's interpretive question.

The saved manuscript proposals contain direct research exposition rather than reviewer acknowledgments or change-status claims. They remain labeled as proposals. The outputs distinguish scientific validity from expression and judge actual concern resolution separately from information preservation.

Two optional wording issues remain in the record. The mathematics answer suggests changing the fixed point to a fixed point while acknowledging that the original phrase need not assert uniqueness. The qualitative reply could state more narrowly that this particular study does not use inter-rater agreement to validate situated themes, avoiding a possible implication about other coding approaches. The assessor did not classify either as a scientific error or failed criterion. Neither output was silently rewritten to erase the caveat.

## Limits

These are deliberately constructed short development cases, not unseen external benchmarks, long manuscripts, independent disciplinary expert certification, or cross-model comparisons. They exercise three skill entrypoints. Structural, license, link, and installation checks cover the full 15-skill library; that does not mean every skill received a new behavioral trial.

There is no unguided control, repeated-sampling estimate, or evidence that the new instructions caused an improvement. Agents used the same configured host with no model override; exact model identifiers were not independently recorded. Study facts are supplied fictional premises, not independently validated data or proofs. No PDF compilation, rendered-page inspection, native Word layout test, reviewer acceptance, or submission check is claimed.
